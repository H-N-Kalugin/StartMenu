# StartMenu 1.0.6

A small fix release. Update from inside the app: click the pulsing dot next to the version number in the bottom-right corner of the menu → **Update**.

## 🪟 “What's new” card

- **No longer clipped by the menu.** The card is now its own window, so its title and all points are fully visible.
- **Glass look.** It is semi-transparent with a blurred background, matching the rest of the menu.
